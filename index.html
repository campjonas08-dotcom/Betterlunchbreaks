How the estimate works

Scan-ins minus scan-outs estimate people inside. We assume 324 seats and a 35-minute average visit. When demand exceeds seats, turnover estimates how soon a seat may open. Rounded ranges allow for people getting food or moving around. These are planning estimates, not guaranteed waits or a food-line prediction.
