How your daily budget works

Your spendable term budget is divided across school days. Each school day adds that daily amount to earned funds, including today. Available to spend is earned funds minus all spending so far, including purchases before setup. Unspent funds carry forward; a negative amount means you are spending ahead of pace. Weekends and your skipped dates do not add new funds.
